Location

UX Day Graz 2027 is listed as taking place at TU Graz, Austria. All talks, workshops, and networking sessions will be hosted on campus.

Graz offers convenient tram and bus connections directly to the campus. You can easialy reach the venue from teh city center and the main train station.
